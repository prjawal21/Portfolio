CH.3: FIELD NOTES

Selected work

Research Internship Mar – May 2026 · Autoliv, Bengaluru

Multimodal road surface classification

Built a system that classifies road surface quality (smooth vs. bumpy) by fusing IMU, GPS, and video. High-frequency IMU signals (~6 kHz) were cleaned with Butterworth bandpass filtering, then paired with a CLIP-based vision pipeline for hybrid labeling. A dual-head CNN combined time-domain and frequency-domain features, reaching ~0.80 ROC-AUC after threshold-optimized false-positive reduction. Road quality was then visualized as GPS-based heatmaps for spatial analysis.
